Idea. The question this answers is the one you actually ask in week 6: "wait — which N are we using, and where did it come from?" Handoffs already carry numbers/decisions/artifacts; nobody re-reads old handoffs. Collected with provenance (task, date, cost) they become a lab notebook the sessions could ALSO read — the context packet's living abstract, generated instead of hand-written. The strikethrough is the one new bit of schema: a supersedes link when a later handoff re-states a number (auto-suggestable by name match). This is the page most unlike a dashboard — and maybe the most valuable.
