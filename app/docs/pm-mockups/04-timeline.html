Idea. A research journal that writes itself: task lifespans + handoff stamps + the heartbeat heatmap reconstruct "what actually happened in July" better than memory does. The striped tails make waiting-on-you time visible as a shape — sur-005's stall is two days, wed-005's is four and growing. All from existing task dates, handoffs, and ledger heartbeats. Cost: vertical space — at ~40 tasks this needs collapsing done rows or a quarter view. Pairs naturally with ⑥ (click a ◇ → its registry entry).
