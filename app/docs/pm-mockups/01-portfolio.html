① Mission control

the portfolio answers one question first: where is your attention owed? — everything else is context
attention owed ranked: waiting-age × what it blocks × deadline proximity
portfolio
Idea. The overview stops being a status board and becomes a triage queue: every waiting question, stalled dependency, and burn anomaly ranked into one list you clear from the top. Project cards demote to context. Everything on this page is derivable today — tasks/*.json (status, waitingSince, lineage, session cost) + plan deadlines + the ledger week. The ranking heuristic is the only new code; no new data, no billed calls. Risk: a bad ranking erodes trust fast — the "why" line under each row is load-bearing, not decoration.
