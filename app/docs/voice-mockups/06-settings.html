Settings › Voice one card in the existing Settings view — voice, pace, and manners

Voice gets one card in the Settings pane you already have (next to theme). Everything runs on the browser's built-in speech — recognition and replies alike. No accounts, no API keys, no new spend, nothing to install: the card is just manners and defaults. (If a better voice ever feels necessary, an external engine could slot in here later — deliberately out of scope for now.)

Cost & privacy. Voice itself is free — no external API, no key, nothing new in the ledger; the only spend a voice message causes is the ordinary Claude turn it sends. Replies are synthesized on this device. One honest caveat: in Chrome, speech recognition routes mic audio through Google's speech service (Safari can do it on-device) — worth knowing, and another reason the mic is push-to-talk rather than always-on.
