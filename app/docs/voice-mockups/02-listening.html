Transcript in the textarea, live. Solid text is finalized; the dim italic tail is the recognizer's interim guess, rewritten in place as it firms up. Because it's the real composer, you can release Space, fix one word by hand, and hit Enter — no separate "voice draft" state to learn.
2
The composer announces listening three ways at once: green ring on the box, glowing filled mic, and a small live waveform tied to actual input level — the waveform is the "is it hearing me?" glance, which matters over a laggy remote connection.
3
The hint line takes the sessbar's row style and teaches the three gestures exactly when they're relevant: release to finish, Esc to throw away, click-to-lock for longer dictation (lock turns push-to-talk into open mic until clicked again). It only renders while listening — zero chrome at rest.
·
Mid-turn dictation is allowed (the turn above keeps running) — same as typing today. Talking does not interrupt Claude's work; only ⏹ stop does. If Claude is speaking aloud when you press the mic, speech output ducks immediately (see mockup 04).
